The Qt libraries are unmodified builds from the official Qt 6.10.1 MSVC 2022

https://code.qt.io/. If you would like the source for the exact files shipped

Replacing Qt: DATAFORM links Qt dynamically. To use a different or modified
build of Qt, put your own Qt 6.10.x DLLs (built with MSVC 2022 for x64, and a
Qt version binary compatible with 6.10) in place of the ones in this folder,
together with the matching plugins and qml/ modules. DATAFORM's source code
is published at https://github.com/Deverydoo/Dataform, so you can also rebuild
it against any Qt version you like.

3. Not included
---------------

LM Studio, OpenAI or Anthropic). No model weights are included. Models you
download through the in-app catalog come with their own licenses, shown in
the catalog.
